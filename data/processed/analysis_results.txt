4. REGRESSION (standard errors clustered by club)
======================================================================
Linear  : Pts ~ wage_share_pct: coef = 5.09 (95% CI 4.34 to 5.84), R2 = 0.610
Log-wage: Pts ~ log_wage_share: coef = 28.29 (95% CI 24.52 to 32.06), R2 = 0.625

Linear: each extra percentage point of the league's total wage bill goes with
about 5.1 extra points. Log version captures diminishing returns:
a doubling of wage share goes with the coefficient x ln(2) extra points (~19.6).
Better fit by R2: log.

======================================================================
CAVEATS TO CARRY INTO THE WRITE-UP
======================================================================
* Association, not causation. Success also drives wages (bonuses, Champions League
  revenue, contract renewals), so wage share partly reflects results, not just buys them.
* Wage data comes from two sources with possibly different definitions of 'wages'.
* Transfer fees ignore wages, loans, free transfers and add-ons.
* ~15 seasons x 20 clubs, with repeat clubs: effective sample size is smaller than n.
